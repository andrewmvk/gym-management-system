# MVP Roadmap (Minimum Viable Scope and Incremental Evolution)

Unit 2 §6 is explicit: **don't ask for Docker, dashboard, authentication, reports, and global tests
in the same prompt**. Build the functional core first, then expand incrementally. This document adapts
the course booklet's generic 5-MVP model to the real FRs in `docs/02-requirements.md`, in the order they
should be implemented - each section is a set of small prompts, not a single prompt.

The concrete prompts for every MVP, with their `Blocked by:` dependencies, MVP stage, and default owners,
are in [`prompts/README.md`](../prompts/README.md). Each prompt states its MVP, so this roadmap and the
catalog stay aligned.

## MVP 1 - Foundation (no AI, no biometrics, no e-mail)

Goal: have something that boots and responds, with real data in the database, with no external
dependency yet.

- Seed script: full `d_user_policy` set + fixed trainer/admin accounts and a demo student member (FR-41, FR-42, FR-43); the `member`/`trainer`/`admin` policy groups join it with P-23 (FR-47).
- Minimal auth: password login, JWT in an httpOnly cookie, basic CASL ability (docs/04-architecture.md §3).
- Catalog: `d_exercises`, `d_gym_equipment`, `d_exercise_equipment` - schema, admin CRUD, listing (FR-16, FR-17, FR-24).

**MVP 1 validation**: a seeded admin can log in, register an exercise and a piece of equipment, and
rule RN-04 (availability) responds correctly in a unit test.

## MVP 2 - End-to-end main flow (AI/e-mail/biometrics still mocked)

Goal: the member's critical path works end to end, even if the "intelligence" of each step is still a
stub for now.

- In-person registration: FR-1, FR-2, FR-9, FR-45, FR-46, FR-57 (real photo capture and recorded LGPD consent, one registration request that also sets the password and creates the member account at once, without enforcing a matching threshold yet). Acceptance of the person (restrictions, any medical certificate) happens at the physical gym before registration and is not part of the system (audit-log item 20); the restriction that the page is only reachable at the gym exists on paper only.
- Login and required onboarding: FR-10 through FR-13 (no real e-mail yet - the login link is exposed via log/console; after login the member is redirected to onboarding, which collects physical information, goals, medications, conditions, structured medical exams and notes).
- First plan: FR-15, FR-18, generated by a **placeholder** generator (e.g., picks 3 fixed exercises), not the real AI.

**MVP 2 validation**: a member goes through registration → login → required onboarding → sees a plan on
screen, with no errors, even though the "brain" of each step is still fake.

The first build of MVP 2 and 3 still implemented a digital aptitude questionnaire, an AI verdict and a
certificate review queue (FR-3 to FR-8). They were removed from the requirements on 2026-10-04
(audit-log item 20) and the code rework is the planned prompt `P-31`, so the bullets above describe the
target, not what the code does until `P-31` is done.

## MVP 3 - Business rules, real AI, and invalid cases

Goal: swap the MVP 2 stubs for the real rules and confirm invalid cases are blocked with a clear
message (this is literally the course booklet's definition of MVP 3).

- Real OpenRouter integration for plan and chat (FR-15, FR-25-29); an AI technical failure is its own error state and never saves a plan (RN-01). The AI purposes are only `plan` and `chat`. The plan is demand-aware (it receives other members' plans per equipment piece and their muscle load, with safety, injuries, medication, exams and muscle focus always winning), and plan safety is part of generation: completed exercises are kept and a confirmation is asked when ticks exist, and a past plan is history (FR-59, RN-06, RN-16, RN-17).
- Remembered facts the member can see and correct: the Health profile page lists every fact, marks one "no longer true", and the chat shows what it just remembered (FR-58).
- Real face recognition (face-api.js) at signup and on the kiosk (FR-2, FR-30-32, RN-08); the kiosk never shows a member's name.
- Check-in + turnstile (FR-33, FR-34, RN-09), with an inactive membership refused at the kiosk (RN-15), and an admin check-in log with turnstile health (FR-61).
- Trainer review (FR-19, FR-21, FR-22, RN-06) and retroactive correction (FR-23, RN-07); trainer notes are readable by any staff member and by the member, the AI uses them, and the review page gives staff the "What the AI knew" card (FR-60).
- Real e-mail via Resend (FR-10).

**MVP 3 validation**: every active RN in `docs/06-business-rules.md` (not the tombstoned RN-02 and
RN-03) has a passing Vitest test.

## MVP 4 - Dashboards and aggregate information

Goal: expose data that already exists and refine the earlier flows; the business rules added here (RN-14
to RN-17) only derive or protect what is already stored.

- Member metrics and membership (FR-36, FR-40): an `inactive` membership locks the member out of login and of the kiosk (RN-15), an admin switches it with a confirmation, and staff get a Member page (FR-60).
- Gym info page: open or closed, hours and the occupancy estimate (distinct members) for everyone, check-ins per hour for staff; equipment and muscle demand lives on the staff Overview as "Today's demand", counted in plans (FR-37, FR-38, FR-39); an admin edits the opening hours (FR-62).
- Muscle map: the muscle registry on every exercise, the plan, history, metrics and gym heat maps, the member's muscle focus and the staff coverage view (FR-49 to FR-52).
- Must-review plans (an exercise that cannot be done flags the plan for the member and for staff), the muscle balance preview on the plan review page, the member Now screen and the staff exceptions Overview (FR-53 to FR-56, RN-14).
- Admin policy management screen with policy groups (FR-43, FR-47, FR-48, RN-10, RN-13).
- Irreversible actions ask for confirmation (switching equipment off shows its impact first, deactivating a membership, regenerating a plan with ticks). These and the features above were refined by the 2026-10-04 critique (audit-log item 21), which amended the prompts it affected instead of adding a new one (implemented, except the browser check).
- In-person registration rework, `P-31` (planned, nothing implemented): brings the registration code in line with the MVP 2 and 3 bullets above by removing the aptitude questionnaire, the AI verdict and the certificate path, registering in one request with a password, requiring onboarding for everyone and adding height, weight and structured exams to it (FR-1, FR-9 to FR-12, FR-44, FR-46, FR-57, RN-12; FR-3 to FR-8, RN-02 and RN-03 removed).
- The coach as the member's trainer, `P-32` (implemented, browser check pending): the chat answers with a full proposal and Apply is a member-triggered mutation with no second AI call, the AI has read-only lookups and no write path, typed blocks (proposal, picker, explainer, safety warning, facts, suggested replies), pointing at exercises and muscles with chips, a draft edited in the browser, safety warnings the member must accept, the member's own edits of sets, reps and weight, injuries on the muscle map, extracted facts confirmed by the member, streamed replies, plan builds and Health profile save, and the trainers' change log and risk flag (FR-21 to FR-23, FR-25, FR-27, FR-28, FR-63 to FR-72, RN-18 to RN-22). Migration 0013 adds `f_profile_events.confirmed_at`, the `manual_plan_edit` event type and `f_plan_changes`.

**MVP 4 validation**: the numbers match what's in the database (verify manually against a direct SQL
query), and the full suite (`pnpm fix`, `pnpm types`, `pnpm test`) is green; the screens amended by the
2026-10-04 critique are checked in a browser once, and `P-31` closes with a manual run of the four signup steps and the required
onboarding.

## MVP 5 - Refinement, tests, Docker, documentation

Goal: close out the course with the Unit 1 §11 final checklist satisfied across the whole prompt
history.

- Docker Compose with the 2 containers the course requires (frontend; backend + PostgreSQL), `.env` variables.
- Test coverage for the remaining RNs that don't have a test yet.
- Sync `docs/`, `rules/`, and `AGENTS.md` with any decision that changed along the way (AGENTS.md §"Keeping docs in sync").
- Fill in `docs/08-traceability-matrix.md` to completion.

**MVP 5 validation**: `docker compose up` brings up the system from scratch and it passes the Unit 1
§11 checklist for every relevant task.
