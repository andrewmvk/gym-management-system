## How to close the open items

Findings 1-5, 11-21 and 23 are closed (the code rework of finding 20 is the planned prompt `P-31`). What stays open are the **implementation decisions**, rows 6-27 of the table above (finding 22 points to rows 11-16, finding 24 to rows 17-22, and rows 23-27 were made after P-32): each is a judgement call made without asking, and the right way to resolve them is a short `/grilling` session focused only on those points (not the whole spec again), because each one is a yes/no question or a choice between options, exactly the format this skill was built to close. Once decided, the answer becomes a new sentence in the relevant FR/RN (or the decision is changed in the code), and that row changes to ✅ with the date and the commit that updated the doc.
